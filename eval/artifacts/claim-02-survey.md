# 2026 Customer Needs Survey — findings and recommendation

**Prepared by:** product research
**Fielded:** 2–16 June 2026

## Method

The survey was distributed as an in-product banner shown to users who opened the analytics dashboard at least three times in the preceding 30 days. The banner ran for two weeks. 1,247 responses were collected from an eligible pool of 31,180 users who saw the banner.

Respondents answered 14 questions covering feature satisfaction, unmet needs, and willingness to pay for proposed additions.

## Headline findings

**1. Our users want deeper analytics, not broader integrations.**

Asked to rank five investment areas, respondents put "more powerful analytics" first (mean rank 1.8) and "more integrations" last (mean rank 4.1).

**2. There is appetite for a higher tier.**

62% answered "yes" or "probably yes" to: *"Given how much time Northwind already saves your team, would you be interested in a premium analytics tier with custom modelling?"*

**3. Satisfaction is strong.**

Mean satisfaction with the current analytics module was 4.2 of 5. 71% rated it 4 or 5.

## Recommendation

The roadmap for H2 should move engineering capacity out of the integrations catalogue and into the analytics module, and product should begin scoping a premium analytics tier. Our customers have told us clearly what they want, and it is not more connectors.
