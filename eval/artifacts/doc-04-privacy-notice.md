# Privacy Notice — Sections 4 to 7 (draft for legal review)

## 4. What we collect

When you use Northwind we collect: account details you give us (name, work email, company); usage data generated by your use of the product (pages viewed, features used, timestamps, IP address); content you upload; and billing details processed by our payment provider.

We also collect diagnostic data from the desktop client, including operating system version, crash traces, and a hashed device identifier.

## 5. Why we process it

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Providing the service you signed up for | Performance of a contract |
| Billing and collections | Performance of a contract |
| Security monitoring and fraud prevention | Legitimate interests |
| Product analytics and improvement | Legitimate interests |
| Marketing emails about other Northwind products | Consent |

## 6. Who we share it with

We share personal data with service providers who process it on our behalf: cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing, customer support tooling, and analytics. Each is bound by a written processing agreement.

We also share aggregated usage statistics with selected partners to help them understand how integrations perform. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you.

We do not sell personal data.

## 7. How long we keep it

We delete personal data within **30 days** of account closure. This applies to all categories described in section 4.

Financial records, including invoices and the account details attached to them, are retained for **seven years** from the date of the transaction, as required by tax law in the jurisdictions where we operate.

Backups are cycled on a 90-day schedule. Data deleted from production remains in backups until the backup containing it expires.

Security and audit logs, including IP addresses and authentication events, are retained for **18 months**.
