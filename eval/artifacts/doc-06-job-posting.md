# Senior Platform Engineer — Northwind

## About the team

The platform team owns the substrate every product team builds on: our Kubernetes fleet, the CI pipeline, and the internal deployment tool **Harbourmaster**, which we open-sourced in 2024 and released version 1.0 of in **March 2024**. Harbourmaster now runs about 4,000 deploys a month.

## What you'll do

- Own reliability for the deployment path end to end.
- Take part in the platform on-call rotation (one week in six, business-hours-first with paging escalation overnight).
- Mentor engineers on the product teams in safe deployment practice.

## Requirements

- 8+ years of professional software engineering experience.
- **5+ years of production experience with Harbourmaster.**
- Deep knowledge of Kubernetes, including operator development.
- Strong Go. We will not consider candidates without demonstrable Go experience in production.
- A track record of owning systems in production, not just building them.

## Nice to have

- Experience running multi-region Postgres.
- Public speaking or writing about platform engineering.

## Compensation and location

Remote within EU time zones. Salary band on request.

## How we hire

Screen, a 90-minute systems design conversation, a paid take-home of about 4 hours, and a final panel. We aim to go from first contact to offer in under three weeks. The right guy will find the process fast and low on busywork.
